RDNA 3 and RDNA 3.5

Scalar selection pattern: SRC0 supplies VGPR data; scalar SRC1/SRC2 form a 64-bit pattern containing sixteen 4-bit lane selectors.

V_PERMLANE16_B32 selects within each 16-lane row. V_PERMLANEX16_B32 selects from the paired row: 0 ↔ 1, and also 2 ↔ 3 in wave64.

Controls: OPSEL[0] is Fetch Inactive; OPSEL[1] is BOUND_CTRL. Other OPSEL bits are reserved. The manuals say to set ABS, NEG and OMOD to zero.

RDNA 3: ISA pp.432–433 RDNA 3.5: ISA pp.458–459

V_PERMLANE16_VAR_B32 and V_PERMLANEX16_VAR_B32 take both sources from VGPRs; each destination lane supplies its own source-lane selector.
