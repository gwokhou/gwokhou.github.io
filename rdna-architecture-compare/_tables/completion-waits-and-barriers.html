S_WAITCNT tests VMcnt, LGKMcnt and EXPcnt against encoded thresholds; each enabled test waits until its counter is at or below the threshold.

No-wait encodings: 0x3f for VMcnt / LGKMcnt, and 0x7 for EXPcnt, disable the corresponding wait.

The counters track outstanding instructions, not threads or a fixed number of elapsed cycles.
